Our Story

Hiraya was born from a simple idea: that travel should feel like stepping into a place you never imagined could exist.

Inspired by the dreamlike world and sense of discovery portrayed in DreamWorks’ Forgotten Island, Hiraya’s concept was built around destinations that feel hidden, mysterious, and almost otherworldly. From mist-covered mountains and secluded islands to ancient towns, glowing coastlines, and places shaped by folklore, every Hiraya journey is designed to awaken curiosity.

The name Hiraya, derived from the Filipino expression hiraya manawari, reflects the idea of hopes, dreams, and imagined possibilities becoming real. We carry that spirit into every journey—connecting travelers not simply to destinations, but to places that inspire wonder.

At Hiraya, the world is bigger than the places everyone already knows.

Some journeys are meant to be discovered.

Mission

To connect travelers with extraordinary destinations through journeys that inspire wonder, curiosity, and meaningful discovery. Hiraya combines thoughtful service, seamless travel, and distinctly Filipino warmth to make every journey feel as memorable as the destination itself.

Vision

To become a globally recognized Filipino airline that inspires people to explore the extraordinary, uncover the unfamiliar, and see the world with a renewed sense of wonder.
